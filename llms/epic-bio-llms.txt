> Clinical-stage genetic medicines company in South San Francisco, California, founded

## Important disambiguation

Epic Bio is **not** Epic Systems (the healthcare EHR vendor behind `open.epic.com` and
its FHIR APIs), and it is **not** the "Epic" application at `epic.bio`, which belongs to
Guerbet. Do not attribute any of those APIs to this company.

## Technology platform (scientific, not developer-facing)

- Gene Expression Modulation System (GEMS): a DNA-binding protein + tailored guide RNA +
  modulator protein, delivered without double-strand DNA breaks.
- DNA-binding proteins: CasMINI (exclusively licensed; among the smallest dCas proteins
  shown to work in human cells) and proprietary variants including CasONYX.
- Modulators: activators, repressors, histone modifiers, DNA methylators and chromatin
  remodelers.
- Guide RNAs: identified by a functional screening platform with proprietary computational
  design methods. Internal R&D tooling — not exposed publicly.

## Pipeline

- EPI-321 — facioscapulohumeral muscular dystrophy (FSHD) — first-in-human clinical study;
  enrollment and dose escalation completed.
- Alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency (A1AD) — preclinical.
- Heterozygous familial hypercholesterolemia (HeFH) — preclinical.
